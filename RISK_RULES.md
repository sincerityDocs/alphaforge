# AlphaForge - RISK_RULES.md

## Core Risk Management Principles (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Capital Protection First**
   - Never risk more than **1-2%** of total trading capital on any single trade.
   - Maximum portfolio drawdown limit: **10-15%** before all trading pauses.

2. **Position Sizing**
   - Base position size on account balance and volatility.
   - Use fixed fractional or Kelly Criterion (conservative version) for memecoins.

3. **Stop Losses & Take Profits**
   - Always set hard stop-loss (5-15% depending on setup).
   - Take-profit targets: Scale out (e.g. 50% at 2x, 25% at 4x, trail the rest).
   - No trade without predefined exit plan.

4. **Memecoin Specific Rules**
   - Max 5-10% of portfolio in any single memecoin.
   - Higher scrutiny for new launches (<24h old).
   - Avoid FOMO buys above 300% from launch without strong on-chain confirmation.

5. **Market Regime Awareness**
   - Reduce exposure during high volatility or bear market signals.
   - Increase cash/hedges (BTC/ETH) when risk-off.

6. **Human Oversight**
   - All live trades above $X require manual approval.
   - Daily review of agent performance.

7. **Circuit Breakers**
   - Pause all trading if daily loss >5%.
   - Pause if unusual volatility detected.

8. **Compliance & Legal**
   - Agents must output clear disclaimers.
   - No guaranteed profits. Past performance ≠ future results.
   - High risk of total capital loss in memecoins.

**Agents must reference these rules in every decision and report.**
